Our Story

A rooftop built for the unhurried evening

Perched above Fatehabad Road, Unplugged Courtyard is Agra's most distinctive dining destination — where craft food, open-air ambience and an unhurried pace come together after 5 PM.

01
Rooftop Courtyard
Open-air seating with a full city view, live plants, and ambient lighting.
02
Craft Brewery
In-house brewed selections alongside a thoughtful cocktail programme.
03
Live Music
Regular live performances — the courtyard comes alive after sundown.
04
Drive-Through & Delivery
Dine in, take away, or get it delivered — no-contact options available.

The Experience

More than a meal —
an evening well spent

Everything at Unplugged is designed to make you stay a little longer, order one more round, and forget you have somewhere else to be.

01
Open Rooftop
A full rooftop courtyard setting with city views, trailing plants, and tables that fill up fast on weekends — arrive early or book ahead.
02
Craft Drinks
An in-house brewery alongside a cocktail list that leans into fresh ingredients — ginger, citrus, herbs — rather than house pours.
03
Live Music Nights
Regular live acts in the courtyard — from acoustic sets to louder Friday nights. Check the Facebook page for schedules.
04
Curated Food
A menu that mixes Chinese-inspired dishes with Indian street classics and open-flame cooking — something for every table dynamic.
05
Dine In & Delivery
Reserve a table for the full experience, or order online for home delivery — no-contact options available every evening.
06
Opposite Trident
Easy to find on Fatehabad Road, directly opposite Trident Hotel — central to Agra's best-known stretch, with parking nearby.
